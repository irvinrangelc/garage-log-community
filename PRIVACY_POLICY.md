# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date: May 29, 2026**

This Privacy Policy explains how Irvin Gersains Rangel Castro ("we," "us," or "our"), the developer of GarageLog ("App"), collects, uses, stores, and shares information about you when you download, install, and use the App. We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your data transparently and responsibly.

By using GarageLog, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree with any part of this policy, please do not use the App.

## 1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

### 1.1 Information You Provide Directly

When you create an account or use the App, you may provide:

- **Account details** — full name, email address, username, and optionally a phone number.
- **Vehicle information** — make, model, year, trim, color, VIN, license plate, odometer readings, and fuel type for each vehicle you add.
- **Maintenance and service records** — dates, mileage, service type, labor cost, parts used, and notes you log manually or via AI scanning.
- **Fuel and expense entries** — fill-up data including date, odometer, quantity, price per unit, station name, and expense category.
- **Scanned documents (Pro)** — photos of fuel receipts and service invoices you submit to the AI scanner feature. These images are transmitted to Google's Gemini AI service for data extraction and are not stored by us after processing.
- **Payment information** — we do not collect or store your payment card details. All billing is processed by Apple through the App Store.

### 1.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you use the App, we may automatically collect:

- **Device information** — device type, operating system version, unique device identifiers, and mobile network information.
- **App usage data** — features accessed, screens viewed, session duration, and interaction patterns, used in aggregate to improve the App.
- **Crash reports and diagnostic data** — error logs and performance data to help us identify and fix bugs.
- **Approximate location** — only if you enable location-based features such as finding nearby service shops. We do not collect precise or continuous background location data.

### 1.3 Information from Third-Party Integrations (Pro)

If you use optional Pro-tier AI scanning features, the following applies:

- **AI scanner data** — photos you submit are transmitted to Google Gemini AI (via Firebase AI) for OCR and data extraction. Extracted data such as amounts, dates, mileage, and service details is returned to the App and stored in your records. Google's handling of submitted photos is governed by Google's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect for the following purposes:

- **To provide and operate the App** — creating and managing your account, storing your vehicle records, and delivering the features you use.
- **To send reminders and notifications** — push notifications, email reminders, and (for eligible Pro subscribers) SMS alerts based on your maintenance schedule and preferences.
- **To enable cloud sync (Pro)** — backing up your data and syncing it across devices linked to your account via Firebase Firestore.
- **To power AI scanning (Pro)** — transmitting receipt and invoice photos to Google Gemini AI to extract structured data for autofilling forms.
- **To generate in-app insights** — calculating fuel economy trends, expense summaries, maintenance patterns, and other analytics displayed within the App.
- **To improve the App** — analyzing aggregated, anonymized usage patterns to enhance features, fix bugs, and inform product decisions.
- **To communicate with you** — responding to support requests, sending important account notices, and (with your consent) product updates.
- **To comply with legal obligations** — retaining records as required by applicable law and responding to lawful requests from authorities.

## 3. Data Storage and Security

### 3.1 Local Storage (All Users)

All user data is stored locally on your device using on-device storage (SQLite database and device storage for attachments). Local data is available offline and is not transmitted to our servers unless you enable cloud sync (Pro). If you delete the App without first exporting your data, locally stored data may be permanently lost.

### 3.2 Cloud Storage (Pro Tier)

Pro subscribers may enable cloud backup, which transmits and stores your vehicle records, service history, and expenses on Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud). We use industry-standard encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest (AES-256). Access is restricted to your account only, and to authorized systems on a need-to-know basis.

### 3.3 AI Processing (Pro)

When you use the AI receipt or invoice scanner, photos are transmitted to Google's Gemini AI service via the Firebase AI SDK for processing. These images are used solely to extract structured data (amounts, dates, mileage, service details) and are not retained by us after the extraction response is received. Google's data handling practices are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

### 3.4 Security Practices

We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

### 3.5 Data Retention

We retain your personal data for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the App's services. If you delete your account, we will delete or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law or for legitimate business purposes such as fraud prevention.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes. We may share your information only in the following limited circumstances:

## 5. Your Privacy Rights and Choices

### 5.1 Access and Correction

You may access and update your account information at any time through the App's profile settings. If you believe any information we hold about you is inaccurate, you may request a correction by contacting us.

### 5.2 Data Export

You may export all of your vehicle records, service history, and expenses at any time from the App's Privacy & Data settings. On iOS and Android, this opens the native share sheet (Files, AirDrop, Mail, etc.); on web, a JSON file is downloaded. This is available to all users, Free and Pro.

### 5.3 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and associated personal data at any time through **Profile > Privacy & Data > Delete Account**, or by contacting us directly. Upon deletion, we will remove or anonymize your personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

### 5.4 Notifications

You may opt out of push notifications at any time through your device's notification settings or within the App's notification preferences. You may opt out of marketing emails by following the unsubscribe link in any email we send. Opting out of service reminders may affect core App functionality.

### 5.5 AI Scanning

Use of the AI scanner features is entirely optional. If you prefer not to have photos transmitted to Google's Gemini AI service, simply do not use the Scan Receipt or Scan Invoice features. All data can be entered manually without using AI scanning.

### 5.6 Analytics Opt-Out

You may opt out of anonymous analytics data collection in the App's privacy settings. Opting out does not affect your ability to use any App features.

### 5.7 California Residents (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you have additional rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including the right to know what personal information we collect, the right to delete your personal information, and the right to opt out of the sale of personal information. We do not sell personal information. To exercise your CCPA rights, contact us using the information in Section 9.

### 5.8 European Users (GDPR)

If you are located in the European Economic Area, you may have rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), including the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict processing of, or port your personal data, and the right to object to processing. To exercise these rights, contact us using the information in Section 9. Our legal basis for processing your data is performance of our contract with you, our legitimate interests in improving the App, and your consent where required.
