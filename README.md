# 🚗 GarageLog — Community & Feedback

> **Car & Bike Service Tracker** · iOS · Free & Pro

GarageLog helps you track vehicle maintenance, log expenses, set service reminders, and keep your entire garage organized — all in one place. This repository is the **public community hub** for bug reports, feature requests, and general feedback.

---

## 📱 About the App

GarageLog is a local-first mobile app built for car and motorcycle owners who want a clean, no-clutter way to stay on top of vehicle care.

**Free Tier**
- Track up to 2 vehicles
- Log services, expenses, and fuel fill-ups
- Date-based and odometer-based reminders with push notifications
- Full service history and expense analytics
- Export all your data as JSON

**Pro Tier**
- Unlimited vehicles
- Cloud sync and cross-device access (Firebase Firestore)
- Real-time vehicle sharing — invite other Pro users as editors
- AI-powered receipt scanning (fuel receipts and shop invoices via Gemini)
- SMS reminder support *(coming soon)*
- Referral rewards — earn a free month of Pro per successful referral

## 🐛 Reporting a Bug

Before opening an issue, please:

1. Update to the latest version of GarageLog from the App Store.
2. Search [existing issues](https://github.com/irvinrangelc/garage-log-community/issues) to see if it's already been reported.

When filing a bug report, please include:

- A clear description of what happened vs. what you expected
- Steps to reproduce the issue
- Your device model and iOS version
- Whether you're on the Free or Pro tier
- Any relevant screenshots or screen recordings

Use the **Bug Report** issue template when opening your issue.

## 🔒 Privacy & Legal

- GarageLog is **local-first** — your data lives on your device by default. Cloud sync is an opt-in Pro feature.
- AI scanning features transmit photos to Google's Gemini AI service for processing and are not stored by us after extraction.
- Vehicle sharing is scoped to Pro users you explicitly invite. You control access and can revoke it at any time.
- For full details, see the [Privacy Policy](https://github.com/irvinrangelc/garage-log-community/blob/main/PRIVACY_POLICY.md) and [Terms of Use](https://github.com/irvinrangelc/garage-log-community/blob/main/TERMS_OF_USE.md).
